The App

🔧 Maintenance

The recurring stuff — oil, electricity, landscaping, home insurance — what it costs every month just to keep the house running, separate from one-time purchases.

How it's different from Items

Maintenance is deliberately simple: a category, a monthly dollar amount, and a Required/Optional flag. It's not a payment log — you're not entering every single oil delivery or electric bill, just the recurring monthly rate. Update the number whenever it actually changes (a new insurance rate, a landscaper price increase); the app doesn't track a history of past payments.

Required vs. Optional

Mark a category Optional if it's genuinely discretionary — lawn care you could do yourself, a cleaning service you could cancel. Flip the Required Only toggle at the bottom of the list to instantly see what you'd actually save by cutting every discretionary cost, without having to do that math by hand.

Adding a category

Click Add Category, give it a name (Heating Oil, Electricity, whatever), a monthly amount, and mark it Required or Optional. Click any existing category to edit or delete it.

Where this shows up elsewhere: your total Maintenance cost feeds directly into Overview's "Cost to Keep" line, and into the monthly comparison table on Sell Scenario & Compare.
